# Independent pilots

Plan only: no participant, installation, consent, repeat use or adoption result is claimed. Issue #13 needs three actual independent Claude Code users, a qualified artifact, and seven-day follow-up. Maintainer demos, agents, CI and downloads do not count. No outreach is authorized by this document.

## Consent and safe records

Explain beta status, actual platform/coverage, ON/readiness/OFF behavior, host fallback and storage limitations before consent. Use synthetic examples; never request live credentials, production transcripts or unrestricted logs. There is no automatic telemetry. Participants may decline, withdraw or omit fields. Agree retention/access and publication consent before collecting feedback; an opaque ID does not guarantee anonymity.

Record only consented safe fields: opaque participant ID, artifact/host/Node/platform version, independent installation result, time to first protected operation, task completion, warning visibility, readiness comprehension, latency category, false-positive disruption, and concrete continuing workflow. Prefer categories rather than incident text; remove identifying paths, secrets and unrelated personal data before publication.

## Day 0

Observe installation, supported synthetic prompt/Read/Bash exercises, readiness failure, both commands and persistent OFF warning. Ask what currently prevents leakage, where failures matter, what would block adoption, and whether they intend to keep protection enabled. Request a concrete continuing workflow rather than a general endorsement.

## Day 7 or later

Record actual elapsed days, repeat use, whether protection remains enabled, and reasons for disabling/uninstalling or host/latency friction. Early replies are not seven-day evidence; missing responses remain missing.

## Decision

The proposed positive learning signal is at least two of three users keeping protection enabled and requesting a concrete continuing workflow. No repeated use, or host/latency friction defeating the task, is a negative signal. These are learning targets, not market-size or security proof.

Complete #13 only with three independent installation records, consented observations, seven-day outcomes and an MCP Beta 1 go/no-go decision based on observed demand plus separately qualified interception feasibility. Do not fabricate missing records or add outreach/feedback automation.
