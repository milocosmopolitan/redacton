## 0.1.0-alpha.1, experimental evaluation

- Added session-scoped `/redacton` and `/redactoff`, captured operation state, readiness, and prompt-area warning UI.
- Added strict textual prompt, Read, and Bash adapters with alias-free reconstruction and an independent outer trusted-result guard.
- Added the pinned local credential helper, strict bounded protocol, native/WASM packaging, and reproducible clean-install checks.
- Recorded actual model-request, storage, synthetic-quality, fault, and resource evidence with platform/version limits.
- Verified normal-terminal OFF warning during typing and after an actual core tool call, resumed/branched ON defaults, same-process OFF scanner bypass, and process-level SIGINT withholding.
- Added MIT licensing, dependency notices, private vulnerability reporting, a maintainer email conduct route, and a pilot plan.

This alpha does not provide production readiness, host-storage prevention, arbitrary host/all-guards failure protection, MCP/PII/binary/all-tool coverage, or independent adoption evidence. A base64 credential miss remains in the curated assessment.
