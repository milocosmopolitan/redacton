# Code of Conduct

This original policy applies to Redacton's issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-associated interactions. It is not a claim of affiliation with another conduct-policy project.

## Expected behavior

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss evidence, code, and product decisions without attacking people. Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Give specific, constructive feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.

Do not post credentials, private transcripts, personal information, or confidential reports. Use synthetic examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and retaliation against a reporter are not acceptable. Deliberately exposing another person's credentials or private information is also prohibited.

Technical disagreement and criticism of a design are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/milocosmopolitan) through an available project channel. For sensitive concerns, ask for a private reporting channel without posting the incident details publicly.

The project currently has no published private conduct-reporting channel. Maintainers must configure one and update this document before a public release. Do not treat an ordinary issue as confidential.

Report security vulnerabilities through the enabled [GitHub private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/milocosmopolitan/redacton/security/advisories/new), following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not put vulnerability details in a public conduct discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate material, request a change in behavior, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant based on severity and repetition. Immediate restrictions may be appropriate for threats or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers should explain decisions privately when practical, limit access to report details, avoid conflicts of interest, and offer a way to request review. No specific response-time or absolute-confidentiality guarantee is made.

This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors.
